Our Manufacturing Heritage

Yorker Engineering Pvt Ltd began operations in 2014 with in-house precision production of measuring tapes. Through rigorous material selection, heavy-duty springs, and clear high-contrast markings, Yorker tapes quickly gained the trust of carpenters, fabricators, and contractors.

Today, Yorker has expanded into complete hardware solutions spanning 293 industrial products across 13 families—including high-torque angle grinders, SDS demolition hammers, marble cutters, diamond saw blades, and abrasives.
