Abrasives & Cutting Technique

Using a zero chipping blade on an angle grinder

Zero chipping blades are designed with a continuous high-concentration diamond rim to give mirror-smooth edges on vitrified tiles, porcelain, and Italian marble. To prevent chipping, maintain an operating speed between 10,000 and 11,000 RPM. Never apply downward force—allow the weight of the angle grinder and the diamond concentration to cut through the material naturally. Always ensure coolant water feed when wet cutting.

Power Tools Selection

Choosing the right hammer drill for your masonry job

For standard wall anchor fixing and brick drilling up to 13mm, Yorker 650W impact drills provide lightweight versatility. For heavy reinforced concrete (RCC), overhead ceiling drilling, and demolition chiseling, always step up to an SDS-Plus or SDS-Max rotary hammer like the Yorker PRO-0835K or Googly PH65. Electro-pneumatic hammer mechanisms generate far greater impact energy (Joules) without operator fatigue.

Precision Engineering

Buying measuring tapes that stay accurate over years of site abuse

Measuring tape inaccuracy typically originates from two failures: rivet loosening on the zero-hook and blade elongation under tension. Yorker measuring tapes utilize high-carbon spring steel coated with anti-abrasive matte nylon film. Triple-riveted true-zero end hooks compensate for both inside and outside measurements accurately throughout continuous industrial usage.
