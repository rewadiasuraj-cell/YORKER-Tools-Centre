Privacy Policy

At Yorker Engineering Pvt Ltd ("Yorker Tools Centre"), we value the privacy of our hardware dealers, industrial contractors, and retail partners. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard dealer inquiry information submitted on yorkertoolscentre.com.

Information We Collect

When you submit a dealer registration or bulk order request, we collect your contact person name, shop/business trade name, phone/mobile number, email address, city/state, GST identification number, and product categories of interest.

How We Use Your Data

Your trade data is solely used to communicate wholesale price lists, send product catalogues via WhatsApp or email, coordinate transport dispatch (Bilti), and verify trade credentials. We do not sell or rent trade contact information to third parties.
