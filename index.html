Why Yorker?

Four reasons contractors, fabricators, and dealers trust Yorker tools.

Engineered for Performance

Pure copper armatures, high-grade alloy gears, and impact-resistant ABS housings designed for harsh Indian job-site conditions.

100% Genuine Specifications

All wattage, RPM, voltage, and cutting diameter ratings are truthful and laboratory-tested under actual load.

Direct Factory Wholesale

Direct manufacturer supply from Chawri Bazar, Delhi with high-margin slabs for distributors and bulk project buyers.

Pan-India Dispatch & Spares

Prompt shipment across all Indian states with comprehensive spare parts availability to keep your tools running longer.
